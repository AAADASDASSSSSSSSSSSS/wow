# STM32F103 — power, decoupling & reset

Guidance specific to STM32F10xxx (Cortex-M3), from ST AN2586.

## Supplies
- Operating supply **VDD = 2.0–3.6 V** (limited to **2.4–3.6 V when the ADC is used**). An embedded regulator produces the internal **1.8 V** digital domain.
- **VDDA/VSSA** must be connected to VDD/VSS respectively; the ADC has an independent VDDA supply that can be filtered separately.
- **VBAT** powers the RTC + backup registers when VDD is off. If no backup battery is used, connect VBAT to VDD (with a 100 nF cap).

## Decoupling (AN2586 §2.2 — authoritative)
- **One 100 nF ceramic per VDD pin**, plus **one bulk cap ≥ 4.7 µF (typ. 10 µF)** tantalum or ceramic for the device.
- **VDDA: 100 nF + 1 µF.** For extra analog noise filtering, VDDA may connect to VDD through a **ferrite bead**.
- **VREF+** (if a separate reference is used): 100 nF + 1 µF; keep VREF+ between 2.4 V and VDDA; VREF+ may connect to VDDA through a ~**47 Ω** resistor.
- Place all decoupling capacitors **close** to their pins.

## Reset
- Integrated **POR/PDR** operates from ~2 V, so **no external reset circuit is required**. NRST is the external reset input; a system reset is generated by NRST low, the watchdogs (IWDG/WWDG), or a software reset.
